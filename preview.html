The Author

Five businesses. Five continents. One thing in common.

I started out in hospitality in Dubai. Eight, ten hour shifts, putting money in someone else's pocket. One day it hit me. I was trading my hours for a life I wasn't going to live. So I left.

Five businesses since.

Online trading funded the lifestyle and the next move. Ecommerce with a team I built myself made money, ran its course, and taught me how to build with other people. Perfume vending machines, pay-per-spray, high-end fragrances in restaurants and clubs across Dubai and Thailand. Still running. A chat support and VA business earned me nothing for three months; I pushed through, sold it clean in December 2024, and walked away with a network I still pull from. Now I'm building EVLVED.

Digital. Physical. Solo. With teams. Sold. Still running. What connected them wasn't a magic skill. It was the willingness to start, the discipline to keep going, and a stack of tools that kept getting better.

22+ countries. Years of travel. Plenty of mistakes, including a $30k loss I won't forget. None of it stopped any of it.

EVLVED is the playbook I'd hand my younger self. Four systems on what I've actually figured out. Discipline. Fitness. Travel. Income. Not theory. The real thing.
