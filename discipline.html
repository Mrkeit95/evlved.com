The 24-Hour Audit.

Before you change anything, you need to know where the hours actually go. The 24-Hour Audit is the first system in the manual because it's the only one that confronts you with the truth.

Duration
7 days
Tool
A notes app, or a physical notebook
Method
Log every block of time over fifteen minutes
Everything. Don't edit it. Don't pretty it up. The point of the audit is to be ugly.
Look For
Where the unaccounted hours go
They hide in two places: the phone and the TV. That's where "I don't have time" lives.

The point. Don't fix everything at once. Pick the single biggest leak and seal it. The audit is not a punishment. It's a map.
