Yes. The system scales. It's built around showing up and progressing, not around already being advanced. Day one matters more than day thirty, and the manual is written to get you to day one.

A gym is ideal, but the system covers training that works in a hotel room and on travel weeks too. What you need is to show up, not a specific set of equipment.

Neither. The target is a lean, conditioned, athletic body. Not chasing mass, not chasing one-rep maxes. The look that comes from training like an athlete.
